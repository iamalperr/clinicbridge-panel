Overview

Plastic, Reconstructive, and Aesthetic Surgical procedures performed at world standards are provided by a surgical team that believes in ethical, contemporary, and sustainable innovation and embraces medical principles, making a difference with their experience and skills…

 

Our surgical center, preferred with confidence in health services, encompasses a total area of 11,800 m2, with 7 floors, 9 operating rooms, 25 observation rooms, and 13 hair transplant units. In addition to plastic surgery, our center also offers services in hair transplantation and treatments, medical aesthetics, dental aesthetics, and IV therapy, providing next-generation health services with its advanced technological infrastructure. Our experienced medical staff and professional team are committed to ensuring that our guests receive quality service.

 

In this project realized with the belief of contributing to our country and benefiting the healthcare sector, under the partnership of 10 plastic surgery specialists, we take pride in achieving a first in the international arena
