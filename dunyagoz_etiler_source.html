Overview

Dünyagöz Hospitals Group continues to grow thanks to the eye health services it provides at high standards with a total of 21 centers in 12 different provinces throughout Turkey. Providing services in 30 different branches of the eye, Dünyagöz has become a pioneer institution in branch hospitals with its world-renowned specialist physician staff.

 

Providing diagnosis and treatment services in the fields of Keratoconus, Presbyopia, intraocular tumor, Bionic eye, Orbita, Retinitis Pigmentosa, and sports eye health, Dünyagöz also provides dietician support for patients with “Diabetic Retinopathy”, one of the causes of blindness due to diabetes, with its 360-degree health solutions.

 

All branches of the eye; cataract, pediatric eye health, laser eye treatment, glaucoma (eye pressure), oculoplasty (aesthetic surgery around the eye), orbital surgery (intraocular tumor surgeries), treatment of retinal diseases and bionic eye treatment that require great experience, all diagnosis, examination and treatment services of eye diseases requiring microsurgical intervention are under one roof.

 

Dünyagöz hospitals continue to serve their patients with the advanced infrastructure used in all centers, a medical staff of 300 professors, associate professors and specialist doctors, 2000 personnel, an average of 8000 polyclinics and 1000 surgeries per day in order to implement the successful treatment approach.

 

We have high-tech devices for the treatment of eye diseases. Some of these are;

MEL90 Excimer Laser
Argon Laser Device
Argon Laser Retina Device
Auto Refractometer Kr Device
iDesign Device
Ifs Visx Star Intralase
Ilasik Devices

 

Dünyagöz Hospitals provides services in 10 different locations outside of Turkey, including Frankfurt and Cologne in Germany, Tbilisi in Georgia, Baku in Azerbaijan, Pristina in Kosovo, Bishkek in Kyrgyzstan, Bosnia and Herzegovina in Sarajevo, Tashkent in Uzbekistan, Haskovo in Bulgaria, and Bucharest in Romania.
