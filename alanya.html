Overview

Opened in 2021 as the 11th branch of Dental Group Hospitadent, Hospitadent Alanya Dental Hospital brings world-class dental care to the heart of Alanya, one of Turkey’s most popular tourist destinations. With a strong and experienced team of dental professionals, the clinic delivers top-quality oral and dental treatments in a modern, comfortable environment.

Our Alanya clinic offers a full range of dental services, including:

Dental Implants

Zirconium Crowns

Digital Smile Design

Laminate Veneers

Box Technique

Bonding Applications

Teeth Whitening

Panoramic X-Ray

Dental Tomography

Located near Gazipaşa Airport and within easy reach of all surrounding resort towns, Hospitadent Alanya is ideally positioned for both local and international patients seeking trusted dental care while enjoying the Mediterranean coast.

✨ Free panoramic X-rays and dental tomography
✨ Complimentary VIP airport transfer service
? Multilingual support in English, German, French, Arabic, Russian, and Bulgarian

Discover the difference in dental care with Hospitadent Alanya Dental Hospital, where expert dentists and state-of-the-art technology come together in one of Turkey’s most vibrant coastal cities.
