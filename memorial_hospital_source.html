Overview

Memorial Healthcare Group (MHG) provides services to both domestic and international patients through its network of 13 hospitals and satellite clinics across Turkey. MHG is a globally recognized brand, particularly in Organ Transplantation, Cardiovascular Surgery, Cardiology, Oncology, and IVF treatments, all delivered at international standards.

 

Memorial is the first hospital in Turkey and the 21st hospital in the world to receive the prestigious JCI (Joint Commission International) Accreditation Certificate.

 

Academic and Expert Medical Staff

  • Over 1,300 physicians, more than 700 of whom hold academic titles
  • More than 7,300 healthcare professionals


 

Our Technologies and Firsts

  • • Advanced oncology technologies (TrueBeam STx, Elekta Versa HD Signature)
  • • First kidney transplant in Turkey performed on a recipient using robotic surgery
  • • Comprehensive prostate and advanced endoscopy centers
  • • An internationally recognized reference center for organ transplantation

 

Memorial Healthcare Group’s technological infrastructure is equipped with cutting-edge medical devices that meet international standards and is designed with a strong focus on patient comfort. Thanks to these advanced technologies, patients experience a safe, comfortable, and efficient diagnosis and treatment process.

 

Memorial Antalya Hospital

Memorial Antalya Hospital offers healthcare services with:

  • • 114 inpatient beds and 28 intensive care beds
  • • 5 operating rooms
  • • Advanced Radiology Department
  • • Intensive Care Units designed with day-night simulation features
  • • Coronary Angiography Unit
  • • Interventional Radiology Unit
  • • Chemotherapy Center
  • • Art Center

 

With its modern architectural design, the hospital combines high-quality healthcare with comfort, offering patient rooms and living spaces comparable to those of a 5-star hotel, positively contributing to patient well-being.

 

Memorial stands by its patients and their families at every stage of treatment and is proud to be chosen by thousands of patients from over 90 countries each year. Most of our staff and interpreters are native speakers, ensuring a smooth and seamless patient experience.


 

Services Offered by Memorial

  • • Second medical opinions and treatment plans prepared by Memorial physicians
  • • Cost estimates for planned treatments
  • • Multilingual staff and interpreter services
  • • Assistance with admission, discharge, and follow-up processes
  • • Organization of air and ground ambulance services
  • • Discounted flight fares and additional benefits through our preferred airline partner, Turkish Airlines
  • • Airport transfer services
  • • Memorial Guesthouse services

• Travel and accommodation arrangements
